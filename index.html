How Hybrid Works

A complete workflow from setup to redeployment, with built-in security and sustainability.

1

Apply NFC label

Quick and easy setup. The NFC label serves as the permanent identifier on your asset.

2

Place Hytraq → Auto Pairing

Automatic connection through seamless NFC integration. No manual configuration required.

3

Real-time Tracking

GPS outdoors for location data. NFC provides Instant Asset Context: Transform the paper chase into 'Tag. Tap. Done.' Access asset history, maintenance records, manuals, and work orders instantly for maintenance teams. Continuous monitoring with sensor triggers.

4

Unpair, Recharge, Reuse → Sustainable Deployment Cycle & Enhanced Resource Recovery

Once removed, immediate unpairing message is sent. Recharge via integrated wireless charging mechanism and ready for the next job. The reusable nature supports a sustainable deployment cycle and contributes to the goal of Enhanced Resource Recovery by maximizing resource utility.

Enhanced Security: For further security, authenticated attachment and removal is possible, by having workers scan a personal NFC card or mobile app.

Recharge and repeat: The tracker is designed for sustainable, temporary deployment cycles, maximizing value and minimizing waste.
